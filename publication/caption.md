**Figure X. Sentinel-1-derived new inundation in Kale Township, Sagaing Region,
Myanmar, between 22 August and 3 September 2026.** (a) Township-wide extent;
(b) location within Myanmar, with Sagaing Region highlighted; and (c) enlargement
of the central valley near Kale. Red denotes the published new-inundation
classification (159.9 km²; 6.85% of the township), and blue denotes the JRC
reference-water mask (seasonality ≥4 months) excluded from this class. The
analysis uses Sentinel-1D IW GRDH VV from two frames on each UTC date, descending
relative orbit 4, obtained through the CDSE Sentinel Hub Process API with
terrain-corrected gamma-zero radiometry. The repository applies a circular
focal mean of nominal 30 m radius to dB backscatter and classifies a post-minus-pre
decrease below −3 dB, retaining slopes below 5° and valid land observations on
both dates. The figure is a lossless reconstruction of the repository's
published classified rasters, not a new end-to-end classification run. Gray
background is post-event SAR; boundaries and the Kale town location are from
MIMU. Main maps use WGS 84 / UTM zone 46N with WGS 84 geographic graticules.
New inundation represents change since the monsoon baseline and does not measure
total standing water or damage. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data
(2026); original analysis © GeoNet Myanmar; administrative and settlement data
© MIMU.

Reference-water source: EC JRC/Google (Pekel et al., 2016,
<https://doi.org/10.1038/nature20584>); terrain exclusion: WWF HydroSHEDS.
